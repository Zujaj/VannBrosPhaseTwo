# Transfer Journals: Review Dispatch & Return

**Who:** Finance / Inventory reviewer  **Where:** Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations

This page explains how to verify the **Transfer** journals that move inventory between locations in **Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations** (company context **VBS | VANN BROTHERS**): the **dispatch** transfer (**INV-ISSUE**) and the **Return Transfer** (**INV-RECV**).

## Background

When inventory is dispatched from the VannBrosPhaseTwo inventory app, the VannBrosPhaseTwo → D365 **batch job** **posts** a **Transfer journal** that moves stock out of the source shed. When leftover stock is returned, the batch job posts a **Return Transfer** journal in the reverse direction. Both live on the same **Transfer** screen under **Inventory Management** and are distinguished only by their journal **Name**.

:::note Review-only flow
You do not enter or post these journals. The VannBrosPhaseTwo → D365 batch job posts them when inventory is dispatched or returned. Your job here is to **verify** what the integration already posted.
:::

## Identifiers at a glance

| Journal | Name | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Transfer (dispatch)** | **INV-ISSUE** | Moves stock OUT of the shed: `CHMCLSHED` → `Staging` |
| **Return Transfer** | **INV-RECV** | Reverse direction: `Staging` → `CHMCLSHED` |

## Steps

1. Go to **Inventory Management > Journal entries > Items > Transfer**.
2. Filter the **Posted** column to **Yes** (operator "is exactly", value **Yes**).
3. Either **search the journal number** (IDs are prefixed `VBS-`, e.g. `VBS-000546`), OR sort the **Journal** column **Z to A** to surface the latest posted journal.
4. Identify the journal type by the **Name** column:
   - **INV-ISSUE** = dispatch.
   - **INV-RECV** = return transfer.
5. Open the journal → **Line details** → open the **Inventory dimensions** tab.
6. Inspect the split between **FROM INVENTORY DIMENSIONS** and **TO INVENTORY DIMENSIONS** to confirm the move direction.

## What to verify

| Column / cue | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Journal** | Journal/voucher ID (prefixed `VBS-`) |
| **Name** | **INV-ISSUE** (dispatch) or **INV-RECV** (return) |
| **Description** | Reads "Item transfer" |
| **Lines** | Lines exist for the expected movement |
| **Posted** | **Yes** |
| **Workflow approval status** | Shown as `None` |

In the **Inventory dimensions** tab, confirm:

- For **INV-ISSUE** (dispatch): **FROM** location is `CHMCLSHED`, **TO** location is `Staging`.
- For **INV-RECV** (return): **FROM** location is `Staging`, **TO** location is `CHMCLSHED`.
- `Site` (e.g. `01`), `Warehouse`, and `Batch number` (`LOT-…`) match across the FROM/TO split as expected.

## Notes & gotchas

:::caution Scope (V1.0)
V1.0 manuals cover only the **review of already-posted journals**. They do not document approval-button clicks, state-transition rules, validation logic, or error messages.
:::

- The **only** differentiator between a dispatch and a return on this screen is the journal **Name** (**INV-ISSUE** vs **INV-RECV**) and the reversed FROM/TO direction.
- The Transfer screen uses the **Posted** column filter, not a **Show** dropdown — set it to **Yes** to see posted journals.
- Item/expense consumption is recorded on different journals — see [Review postings](./review-postings).
- A returned item can also appear as a **negative-quantity line** on the Item Journal — see [Review a Return Item Journal](./review-return-item-journal).
